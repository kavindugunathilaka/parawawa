FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions & Answers

Find immediate technical answers about Parawewa eco bio septic tank systems.

How does Parawewa compare to imported mechanical STPs in Sri Lanka?

Unlike imported mechanical STPs which require continuous 24/7 electricity to run air blowers and need frequent mechanical servicing, Parawewa engineered by Parawewa provides two flexible options: Option A (100% Gravity-Fed with no electricity for normal ground) and Option B (Seal Type Parawewa System for high ground water table), both featuring a 10-Year written manufacturer guarantee and no gully bowser emptying.

What is the difference between Option A (Gravity) and Option B (Seal Type Parawewa System)?

Option A (Gravity-Fed) operates on 100% passive natural gravity flow with no electricity, ideal for standard ground levels. Option B (Seal Type Parawewa System) is a water-sealed system designed specifically for high water table lands (such as Negombo, Ja-Ela, Wattala or Mathugama) where natural gravity discharge is restricted. It uses some electricity, but less than mechanical STPs.

How does the 10-Year Manufacturer Warranty work?

Parawewa issues an official 10-Year written manufacturer guarantee certificate covering structural tank integrity, chamber sealing, and multi-stage biological layer filtration performance.

Can Parawewa be installed in high water table and marshy lands?

Yes! Parawewa Option B is specifically engineered for high groundwater tables, wetlands, and coastal soils (Negombo, Ja-Ela, Wattala, Mathugama, Galle). The hermetically sealed concrete body prevents inward water seepage and flood backflow.

Does Parawewa comply with Central Environmental Authority (CEA) standards?

Yes, Parawewa effluent discharge is laboratory-tested to meet all Sri Lanka Central Environmental Authority (CEA) discharge guidelines (BOD & COD limits), making it suitable for commercial buildings, hotels, and apartments.

Who is the owner and inventor of Parawewa?

The Parawewa bio septic system was invented in 1996 by Ranjith Dharmakeerthi Mannage, Managing Director of Parawewa in Sri Lanka. The technology holds Sri Lanka Patent #16371.

What size Parawewa tank does my property need?

Recommended capacity depends on your property type and number of occupants. Use our interactive tank sizer above for an instant approximate estimate, then request a free site inspection from Parawewa for an exact recommendation and quote.

Is Parawewa suitable for factories, hotels, and commercial buildings, or only homes?

Both. Parawewa is installed across residential, industrial, and commercial sites islandwide — including factories, retail outlets, and housing developments — with capacity sized to each site's occupancy and wastewater load. See our real project case studies for examples.
