In 1996, Mr. Dharmakeerthi Mannage set out to solve one of Sri Lanka's most persistent sanitation problems: leaky septic tanks, foul odor contamination, and the high recurring cost of gully bowser trucks.

Through years of biological research, he invented the patented Parawawa (Parawewa) layer-base bio septic system. His innovation earned 1st Place at the 2018 Presidential Innovation Awards in Sri Lanka and a Silver Medal at iCAN 2019 in Canada.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions & Answers

Find immediate answers about Parawewa eco bio septic tank systems.

How does Parawawa compare to imported mechanical STPs in Sri Lanka?

Unlike imported mechanical STPs which require continuous 24/7 electricity to run air blowers and need mechanical servicing, Parawawa operates with 0% electricity, zero moving mechanical parts, 100% passive bio-filtration, and includes a 25-year manufacturer guarantee.

What do verified customers say about Parawawa bio septic tanks?

Over 25,000 Sri Lankan homeowners and developers rate Parawawa 4.9/5.0. Clients report zero gully bowser visits for over 10 years and complete elimination of sewage odor.

Who is the owner and inventor of Parawawa (Parawewa)?

The Parawawa (Parawewa) bio septic system was invented in 1996 by Mr. Dharmakeerthi Mannage, Chairman and Managing Director of Buddija Construction / Intelligence Constructions in Sri Lanka. The technology holds Sri Lanka Patent #10848.
