What is Option B?

Option B is the Parawewa configuration for sites where natural gravity discharge is restricted. It uses the same patented biological treatment as Option A, in a hermetically sealed concrete body, and adds a heavy-duty submersible pump that lifts the treated water to the absorption point where it can be discharged safely.

Why high water table land needs a different design

In low-lying coastal and wetland areas, the ground is saturated for much of the year, and heavy monsoon rain raises the water table further. Standard concrete rings were never designed for that:

  • Porous, jointed concrete can take in groundwater and fill up shortly after installation.
  • Hydrostatic pressure pushing up from below can shift or float a tank, a problem known locally as “tank floating”.
  • On flat or low plots there is no fall for effluent to leave the tank by gravity.

See the full engineering guide: Installing bio septic tanks in high water table lands.

How Option B is engineered for these sites

  • Sealed concrete chamber. A one-piece, in-situ cast concrete chamber, rather than jointed rings or brickwork, prevents inward water infiltration even when the tank sits below the local water table.
  • Discharge to the absorption point. A heavy-duty sealed pump lifts the treated water to the absorption point where gravity discharge is not possible.
  • Shallow, wide geometry. Wide, shallow tank geometry avoids deep excavation into saturated ground, reducing excavation risk and dewatering cost.
  • Anti-buoyancy anchoring. The installation is anchored and ballasted to resist uplift from groundwater pressure.

Where Option B is used

Option B is specified for high groundwater table lands, marshy wetlands, coastal properties and flat elevations, including areas such as Negombo, Ja-Ela, Wattala, Mathugama and Galle.

Warranty and treatment performance

Option B carries the same 10-year written manufacturer warranty as Option A, covering structural tank integrity, chamber sealing and multi-stage biological layer filtration performance. The biological treatment is the patented the Parawewa biological process (Sri Lanka Patent #16371), with effluent laboratory-tested against CEA discharge guidelines.

Option A or Option B?

If your ground is standard and your water table is normal, Option A (gravity-fed) avoids the pump and uses no electricity. If your site is waterlogged, coastal or flat, Option B is the right fit. A free site inspection confirms which applies.

Frequently asked questions

Can Parawewa be installed in high water table and marshy land?

Yes. Option B is engineered for high groundwater tables, wetlands and coastal soils such as Negombo, Ja-Ela, Wattala, Mathugama and Galle. The hermetically sealed concrete body prevents inward water seepage and flood backflow.

Does Option B use electricity?

Yes. Option B uses some electricity, less than a mechanical STP, to lift the treated water to the discharge point. Option A, the gravity-fed model, uses no electricity and is the choice for standard ground.

Will the tank float in waterlogged ground?

The installation is anchored and ballasted to resist uplift from groundwater pressure, and the tank uses a shallow, wide geometry that avoids deep excavation into saturated soil.

What is the difference between Option A and Option B?

Option A operates on passive gravity flow with no electricity and suits standard ground levels. Option B, the Seal Type Parawewa System, suits high water table land where gravity discharge is restricted. Both use the same patented biological treatment and carry a 10-year written warranty.

What does the 10-year warranty cover?

Parawewa issues a 10-year written manufacturer guarantee certificate covering structural tank integrity, chamber sealing and multi-stage biological layer filtration performance.

Not sure which option fits your plot?

Request a free site inspection. The Parawewa team will confirm whether Option A or Option B suits your ground and recommend a tank size for your property.
